# SPRITES.md — LANTERNVALE asset provenance

Every visual asset in `assets/` is listed here with its source. Rule: original
code-drawn art first; external assets ONLY from Kenney (CC0), OpenGameArt
(CC0/CC-BY, never GPL/NC/SA), or CraftPix free section. No hotlinking —
everything is bundled locally.

## Art direction (committed 2026-10-15)
**"Flat storybook with soft air"** — chosen after rendering the key vista in
3 directions (flat / textured-pixel / soft-pastel). Flat won for clarity and
cozy charm; soft air (gradient sky, subtle paper grain, warm wash, vignette)
keeps it from feeling stark. 28-color golden-hour palette (`art/artcore.py`
`PAL`). Pixel art is integer-scaled nearest-neighbor in-game. Rejected:
textured-pixel (dither read as dirt), soft-pastel (blurry at sprite sizes).
