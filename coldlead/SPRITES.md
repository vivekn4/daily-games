# COLD LEAD — Sprite/Asset Credits

**100% original, code-drawn. Zero external assets.**

Every visual in COLD LEAD is generated at runtime by `js/sprites.js` —
no image files, no downloads, no licenses to clear:

- **Suspect portraits** — procedural seeded pixel portraits (skin tone,
  hair style/color, eyes, brows, mouth, facial hair, hats, glasses,
  beauty marks, coats/ties/scarves). Deterministic per suspect: the same
  seed always draws the same face. 6 named characters (Reyes, Marlow, Venn,
  the Composer) use fixed seeds for consistent identity.
- **Crime scenes** — 6 painter functions (jazz club, theater, warehouse,
  hotel, museum, night train): neon signs, venetian blinds, spotlights,
  crates, marquee bulbs, rain-slick streets. All drawn with canvas
  primitives, seeded per scene.
- **VFX** — rain particle system, film grain, neon flicker, fog bands,
  spotlight cones, light sweep. The signature noir look is code, not assets.
- **Clue icons** — drawn glyphs for physical/forensic/testimony/document
  clues (envelope, fingerprint, speech marks, paper, key).
- **Audio** — 100% WebAudio-synthesized original SFX set (see `js/audio.js`):
  rain loop, thunder, typewriter ticks, phone ring, walking-bass jazz trio,
  siren, paper shuffle, ui blips, sting, solved chime, fail drone, dawn
  bell, heartbeat. Nothing recycled from Emberdeep.

No Kenney, no OpenGameArt, no third-party anything — nothing to credit,
nothing to attribute.
