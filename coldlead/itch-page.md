**Title:** COLD LEAD — Night Shift Detective
**Tagline:** Five nights. Five bodies. One truth. A procedural noir detective game.
**Genre:** Mystery / Detective — **Tags:** detective, noir, mystery, procedural-generation, crime, singleplayer

## Description

Grayharbor. 11:47 PM. Rain like static on a dead channel.

You are the new night-shift detective, and a body just dropped under the
pier lights. You have until 06:00 to name the killer — the *right* killer.

**COLD LEAD** is a procedural noir detective game: every night is a fresh,
seeded case — new victim, new scene, 4–6 suspects with motives, alibis and
secrets, and exactly one culprit. Search rain-slick crime scenes, run
forensics through Dr. Venn's lab, and interrogate suspects across five
topics with three approaches: **press** them, **empathize** with them, or
**bluff** with the evidence. Pin clues to suspects on the deduction board —
but you need two strong links to make an accusation stick, and a wrong name
means the case goes cold.

Threaded through the five nights is the **Pale Composer**, a killer who
leaves white piano keys where bodies fall… and who has started leaving them
for *you*. Three endings.

- 🎲 Fully seeded procedural cases — same seed, same case. Share seeds, race friends.
- 🕵️ Interrogation chess: 4 suspect personalities × 3 approaches × 5 topics
- 🧪 Forensic lab queue running in real background time
- 📌 Deduction board with evidence pinning and link requirements
- 🌧️ Signature noir presentation: rain, neon, venetian blinds, film grain
- 🎷 Original WebAudio jazz-noir soundscape (synthesized, zero assets)
- 🔊 Voice narration toggle + typewriter text (3 speeds)

## Controls

- **Mouse / touch:** click or tap everything
- **Keyboard:** 1–9 select · arrows + Enter navigate · B deduction board · Esc pause · M mute

**Seed:** shown on the results screen — replay any case exactly.
