## Title
Emberdeep

## Tagline
A tiny roguelike dungeon quest. Ten floors. One ember. Three endings.

## Description
The Ember that keeps your village warm has been stolen by the Hollow King,
ten floors down in the dark. You are Ash, the lantern-keeper's apprentice —
not a hero, just the only one who volunteered.

**Emberdeep** is a complete roguelike dungeon RPG in your browser:

- **10 procedurally generated floors** — no two runs are the same (every run
  has a seed; retry your favorite seeds or roll fresh ones)
- **7 enemy types** with distinct behaviors — chargers, kiters, splitters,
  dashers, turrets — plus elite variants
- **4 bosses**, one every three floors, each with its own patterns and taunts
- **Build your Ash**: XP levels with 1-of-3 upgrade choices, 5 tiers of
  weapons (burning blades, lifesteal edges…), 5 passive charms, potions and bombs
- **A real story spine**: intro, an NPC with a quest hook, boss taunts, and
  **3 different endings** decided by your final choice
- **60–90+ minutes** per full run; permadeath, new seeds, and alternate builds
  make every run different

No downloads, no accounts — runs entirely in your browser. Free.

## Controls
- **WASD / Arrow keys** — move
- **SPACE** — attack (hold)
- **Q** — drink heal potion · **E** — throw bomb
- **1 / 2 / 3** — choose level-up gifts and dialogue options
- **M** — mute · **P / Esc** — pause
- **Touch:** left d-pad to move, ⚔ to attack, 🧪/💣 for potions
