# EMBERDEEP — Story Script

## Premise
The village of Cinderhold survives the mountain winter on the warmth of the
Ember, a crystal the founders pulled from the deep earth generations ago.
Three nights past, the Hollow King — the thing that wears the dungeon
Emberdeep like a crown — rose up and took it. Without the Ember, Cinderhold
freezes by winter's end.

You are **Ash**, seventeen, the late lantern-keeper's apprentice. You are not
a hero. You are simply the only one who volunteered.

## Intro crawl (in-game dialogue)
> The Ember is gone. Without it, Cinderhold will freeze by winter's end.
>
> The Hollow King took it deep into Emberdeep. Ten floors down.
>
> **Ash:** Then I'd better get walking. Keep a lantern lit for me.

## Floor 1 — Old Maren (NPC quest hook)
> **Old Maren:** Ash! You actually came down. Brave or boiled, I can't tell.
>
> **Old Maren:** Listen: swing with SPACE. Walk into chests to open them. Stairs go down.
>
> **Old Maren:** Take my old lantern — it drinks the dark. And come back alive, you hear?
>
> *(receives the Old Lantern charm: +light radius, +magnet range)*

## Boss taunts
- **Gloom Matriarch (floor 3):** "MY CHILDREN HUNGER, LITTLE LIGHT." / "THE DARK HAS TEETH. I AM ITS JAW."
- **Cinder Archon (floor 6):** "BURN WITH ME, LANTERN-BEARER." / "YOUR EMBER IS ALREADY MINE."
- **Hollow Warden (floor 9):** "TRESPASSER. THE KING DOES NOT BARGAIN." / "KNEEL, OR BE KNELT."
- **The Hollow King (floor 10):** "YOU CARRY LIGHT INTO MY THROAT, CHILD?" / "I WAS A KING WHEN YOUR VILLAGE WAS SPARKS." / "ENOUGH. DROWN IN THE DARK."

## The choice (after the Hollow King falls)
The Ember lies where the throne was. It pulses like a heart. Three options:

1. **Return it** — carry it home.
2. **Keep it** — the power is yours; why give it back?
3. **Destroy it** — dash it on the throne and end the whole hungry cycle.

## Endings
- **Dawn:** You lift the Ember high and the dark recoils. Cinderhold glows again
  by winter. Old Maren weeps into her soup. The dungeon seals itself behind
  you, and for the first time in years, the lanterns stay lit.
- **Emberlord:** Power hums in your palm, warm as a second heart. Why give it
  back? You turn from the stairs and descend deeper, the dark parting like a
  crowd. Somewhere above, the lanterns go out one by one.
- **Release:** You dash the Ember on the throne and the Hollow King exhales — a
  sound like wind through a door. The dark thins, ordinary and quiet. No kings,
  no embers, no more dungeon. Just morning.

## Death text
> The dark keeps the ember. The village keeps waiting.
> *(shows run stats + seed; "New run" / "Retry seed")*
